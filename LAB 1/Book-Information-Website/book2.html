📖 Book Summary

Wings of Fire is an inspirational autobiography of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, the former President of India and renowned aerospace scientist. The book covers his early life, struggles, hardships, fortitude, and the chance opportunities that led him to lead India's space research, missile development, and nuclear programs. It is an uplifting story of courage, perseverance, and dedication to nation-building.
