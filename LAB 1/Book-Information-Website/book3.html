Rich Dad Poor Dad

by Robert Kiyosaki

Price ₹399
Published 1997
Publisher Plata Publishing
Overall Rating 4.7 ★

📖 Book Summary

Rich Dad Poor Dad advocates the importance of financial literacy, financial independence and building wealth through investing in assets, real estate investing, starting and owning businesses. It contrasts the financial mindsets of the author's biological father (the 'poor dad', a highly educated government worker) and his childhood best friend's father (the 'rich dad', an entrepreneur).
